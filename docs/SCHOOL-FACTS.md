## Keremet school fact sheet (ТОО "Keremet-City", Shymkent)

I checked the key claims myself on 24.09.2026: the 2GIS card and its info tab, the Instagram profile, the Instagram admission post of 12.08.2025, and ba.prg.kz. The web search budget ran out, so nothing else was re-checked.

- **Names:** «КЕРЕМЕТ» ЗИЯТКЕРЛІК МЕКТЕБІ, Частная школа «Керемет» / Keremet, жеке мектеп. Sources: Instagram display name, the 12.08.2025 post, the 2GIS card.
- **Where the school operates:** г. Шымкент, Абайский район, мкр. Асар, 911/2. Sources: 2GIS and the Instagram post.
- **Legal entity:** ТОО "Keremet-City", БИН 210440038887, registered 28.04.2021. Legal address: 160000, мкр. Асар, ул. Сейхун, зд. 125. Director: Караманова Диана Муратхановна. Activity code (ОКЭД) 85310. Sources: the user's documents and ba.prg.kz.
- **Licenses:** KZ18LAA00032760 (07.11.2022) and KZ29LAM00002781 (05.05.2025). Source: the user's documents only.
- **Main phone:** +7 (771) 242-45-38, also on WhatsApp at wa.me/77712424538. Sources: 2GIS and the Instagram post.
- **Instagram:** @keremet_mektep_asar, about 405 followers. The 2GIS card links to it.
- **Grades and languages:** "0-6 сыныптарға арналған мектеп (KZ/RU)". Features listed: Кәсіби педагогтар, Тегін үйірмелер, Ұзартылған күн, Ыстық тамақ. Source: Instagram bio.
- **Admission post (12.08.2025):** it says the school takes pupils into the primary grades and offers free tuition in Kazakh and Russian. Programmes listed:
  - teaching by the НИШ/РФМШ methods
  - Singapore maths
  - in-depth and olympiad maths
  - in-depth language study
  - full-day school and extra lessons
  - public speaking and financial literacy
  - sports clubs and programming
  - robotics and AI
- **Building:** 2 storeys, a ramp and an accessible entrance, 7 parking spaces, 250 m (3 min walk) from the проспект К. Жалаири stop. Source: 2GIS.
- **Rating:** 4.8 from 241 ratings and 52 reviews. Source: 2GIS.
- **Hours:** opens at 09:00. Source: 2GIS.

**How the entity is tied to the school:** 2GIS names the legal entity "ТОО Керемет", not Keremet-City. We treat them as the same school because the user's documents say so. The same microdistrict and district, the same activity code and the director's Keremet brand all support this. Nothing online states it directly.
